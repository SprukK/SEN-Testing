    Range

    Stability along the range

    Bands: top edge = no spin decay, bottom edge = spin decay with the assumed C_lp. Sg rises downrange because velocity falls faster than spin. EST Downrange Sg is not yet anchored to an external reference (§20A) — the muzzle values are the verified ones.

    What is calculated and what is drawn

    verified velocity, Mach and time of flight (the calculator's RK4 engine), Miller Sg at the muzzle (= Berger), McGyro Sg at the muzzle, BC penalty, mass from geometry — copied unchanged from Main V2.16.3.
    EST spin rate downrange (roll damping C_lp assumed), Sg downrange, Reynolds number (Sutherland viscosity), epicycle frequency ratio (linear theory, McCoy).
    illustrative streamlines, boundary layer, transition at the engraved band, separation point, wake size, yaw size (initial disturbance assumed; no damping until dynamic stability is built), engraving count. Display spin is slowed down; the real rate is in the tiles.
