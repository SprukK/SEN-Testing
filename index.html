SEN · BLE Probe V1.0.0

Generic BLE diagnostic for the SEN platform. Built for the Calypso Mini AB variant comparison (WDOT-B1F9A6 works / new unit connects but no wind), reusable for TITAN 3K and the F5 e-ink peripheral. Chrome on Android or desktop, HTTPS only. Separate from the calculator: no release churn.
